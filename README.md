# Russian placement tests, TORFL-style (A1 / A2, English or French)

Click-only (no typing, no Cyrillic keyboard). Pick a language, a level, then an exam from the bank.

## Structure (follows the TORFL subtests that can be tested with multiple choice)
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Vocabulary & Grammar | vocabulary matching (RU→EN/FR and EN/FR→RU) + sentence completion (forms, cases, aspect) |
| Reading | short texts (notes, ads, messages): multiple choice and true/false |
| Listening | 2 plays per recording: questions, short dialogues, announcements, best-reply items |

The real TORFL also has **Writing** and **Speaking** subtests; they are not covered here.

## Scoring
Each section is scored **corrected for guessing** (wrong answer = -1/(options-1)); the overall level is your **weakest** section.
<35% below level · 35-65% working towards · 66-79% borderline pass · 80-91% secure · 92%+ very strong.

## Honest limits
- AI-generated, **not psychometrically calibrated, not official**; expect about ±8 points of noise (more on the shorter exams).
- TORFL's pass mark is 66% per subtest (confirm with your testing centre); aim for 80%+ for a margin.
- Cross-check with official sample papers (SPbU, RUDN, Pushkin Institute, MSU).
- Different exams reduce memorisation: take a new one rather than retaking the same one.
